About this page

Everything here stays in this browser on this device — your clothes, your photos, your notes. Nothing is sent to LoveGPT, to me, or to anyone. Clearing your browser data will erase it, so download a backup now and then.

The only thing this page ever fetches from the internet is the weather, from Open-Meteo. It receives a rounded location (to about a kilometre) and nothing else — no account, no identifier, no cookie. Pick a city instead if you’d rather the browser never asks where you are.

Suggestions come from a small set of rules about the weather and the day, applied to the clothes you already own. No AI, nothing invented.
