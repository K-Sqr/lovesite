**Key responsibilities**

* Research and Development Support  
* Assist Researchers: Provide technical and administrative support to researchers in the planning, execution, and analysis of teaching and learning experiments and other studies.  
* Laboratory Management: Oversee the daily operations of the laboratory, ensuring all equipment is maintained, updated, and calibrated (e.g., VR headsets and associated computers). Allocates spaces for research projects.  
* Data Collection and Analysis: Collect, manage, and analyze experimental data, ensuring accuracy and reliability.  
* Drafting: Organizes results, prepares drafts for publication and co‑authors papers.  
* Training and Development: Conduct training sessions for students training in the laboratory on protocols and equipment usage in the Science of Teaching and Learning Laboratory.  
* Safety Compliance: Ensure all laboratory activities comply with safety regulations and protocols.  
* Resource Management: Manage laboratory supplies and inventory, including ordering and stocking necessary materials.  
* Documentation: Maintain detailed records of experiments, procedures, and results for future reference and reporting.  
* Collaboration: Work closely with Portfolio Lead – Data, Evaluation, and Higher Education Research, Faculty Specialist – Science of Teaching and Learning, and other team members to facilitate collaborative projects and initiatives.  
* Technical Support: Submit support tickets when technical support is required for laboratory equipment and software to IST and external vendors.  
* Innovation and Improvement: Identify opportunities for improving laboratory processes/manuals and implementing new technologies or methodologies.
