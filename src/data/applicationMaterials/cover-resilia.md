**Application for the Position of a Full-Time Case Manager** 

Dear Hiring Committee,

I am writing to apply for the full-time Case Manager position at Resilia Community Wellness Centre. I served as a Newcomer Case Manager in the RNCM program from January until my fellowship term ended in August, and I am applying to return to the team in a sustained, full-time capacity.

During my term, I carried my own caseload of refugee individuals and families. I conducted eligibility assessments within Resilia criteria, produced triage reports, and developed case plans that identified and managed risk factors, reassessing need levels as clients’ circumstances changed. I supported clients through home visits and interpreter-assisted conference calls and coordinated referrals and accompaniment across service providers, including EIA, Manitoba Housing and Rent Assist, Manitoba Start, Pluri-elles, the Immigrant Centre, and health, education, and child care services. Every contact was documented in CARMIS, including case notes, genograms, family ecomaps, and the service activity records that feed program statistics and iCARE reporting.

That operational familiarity is what I would bring on day one. I know the RNCM zones and eligibility criteria, how a high-needs file is carried differently from a low-needs one, how detailed case notes can influence action, and which partner organization to call for which need. I know the team, having been trained by and worked alongside the coordinator and the case managers across the program, and I have already established working relationships with the clinicians and community partners this role depends on.

My understanding of trauma is both trained and researched. I hold Crisis Counselling Skills Training through Klinic and HRPP-E Facilitator certification, and my undergraduate honours thesis examined alexithymia and attention-emotion interactions, work concerned directly with how trauma disrupts emotional processing. Through IRCOM’s Newcomer Youth Participatory Action Research Project, I interviewed refugee and immigrant youth about the barriers they face in the education and justice systems. What that research taught me, and what my time at Resilia confirmed, is that hypervigilance, guardedness, and mistrust of institutions are not obstacles to case management. They provide information about what a client has survived and about the pace the work needs to take.

The posting also calls for group facilitation and the ability to take initiative under limited supervision. As the convener and curator of Meeting Rapha: Exploring Loss, Grief & Healing, I designed and delivered a community program on grief and trained and coordinated more than 25 volunteers across five teams in mental health first aid and de-escalation. At Resilia, I initiated and closed two gaps on my own: the RNCM Resource Guide, a comprehensive directory of the program’s referral and resource information, which now supports staff onboarding, and a multi-level English Language Pre-Class Support for clients experiencing a 6 to 12 month waitlist for government-funded language classes.

My B.A. Honours in Psychology, combined with over two years of cross-cultural work with refugee and immigrant communities, gives me both the theoretical grounding and the practical case management experience this position requires. I am available to start immediately and open to the occasional evening and weekend hours the role involves. I would be glad to continue this work with Resilia and to grow into the full scope of the position. Thank you for your time and consideration.
